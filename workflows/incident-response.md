# Workflow: Incident response

1. Establish incident commander/owner, severity, timeline and communication channel.
2. Protect users and data first; contain impact without destroying evidence.
3. Identify affected services, tenants, data classes, integrations and time window.
4. Preserve relevant logs/metrics/traces securely; avoid copying secrets or unnecessary personal data.
5. Mitigate using approved runbooks; obtain authorization for risky changes.
6. For suspected credential exposure, rotate/revoke affected credentials through approved procedures.
7. Assess notification, privacy, contractual and regulatory obligations with responsible humans.
8. Restore service and verify critical journeys and data integrity.
9. Conduct blameless post-incident review: timeline, root causes, detection gaps, actions and owners.
