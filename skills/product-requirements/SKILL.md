# Product requirements

## Procedure
1. Identify target users, problem, current alternatives, and evidence.
2. Separate user-stated facts from assumptions and hypotheses.
3. Define goals, non-goals, MVP scope, and explicit exclusions.
4. Map primary journeys, roles, permissions, and edge cases.
5. Define functional and non-functional requirements.
6. Define measurable success metrics and guardrails without inventing targets.
7. Capture integrations, legal/privacy constraints, operational needs, and dependencies.
8. Write acceptance criteria in observable, testable language.
9. List open questions and decisions requiring stakeholder approval.

## Do not
- Invent customer research or market validation.
- Turn every idea into an MVP requirement.
- Promise delivery dates without estimates and dependencies.
- Treat assumptions as confirmed decisions.
